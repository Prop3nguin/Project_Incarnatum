# The Aetherium 

## [Taluria](/Taluria/README.md)
 
### [Incarnatum](/Incarnatum/README.md)
Incarnatum is the first Narrative in the works for the Aetherium. The story takes place on a planet called Taluria, home to humidity and an extremely stable climate, the people there are under threat from a faraway being of unknown origin who works in the shadows to pull the pieces into just the right place... 

> [!note]
> This is a Narrative work that is still in progress.
